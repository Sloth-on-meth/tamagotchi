# Bitling

A pocket virtual pet that lives in your browser, in the style of the classic 90s keychain pets. Its characters and art are original.

Open `index.html` in a browser. There's no build step and nothing to install.

- Hatch an egg and raise it from baby to child, teen and adult. There are 4 possible adult forms, and which one you get depends on your care.
- Feed it meals or snacks, play the left/right guessing game, clean up poop, give medicine and turn off the light at bedtime.
- Discipline it when it misbehaves.
- Time keeps passing while the page is closed. Progress is saved in `localStorage`.
- Controls: buttons A (select), B (confirm) and C (cancel), or the keyboard keys `A`/`S`/`D`. You can also tap an icon twice.
- Speed settings of 10× and 60× are there for faster play and testing.
