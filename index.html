How to play
  • A moves through the icons, B uses the selected one, C cancels. You can also tap an icon twice. Keyboard: A/S/D or 1/2/3.
  • Food: meals fill hunger hearts; snacks cheer it up but add weight. Too many snacks can make it ill.
  • Play: guess which way it will hop (A = left, B = right). Win 3 of 5 rounds for a happy heart.
  • Clean up poop quickly, and give medicine when you see the skull.
  • It sleeps at night: turn the light off so it can rest.
  • When it acts up for no reason, use discipline. Scolding it for nothing just makes it sad.
  • The bell lights up when it needs you. Ignoring it for 15 minutes counts as a care mistake. How well you look after it decides what it grows into.
  • Press B with nothing selected to see the clock. Time keeps passing while the page is closed.
